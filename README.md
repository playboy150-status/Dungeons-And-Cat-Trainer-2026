## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Dungeons And Cat**. This trainer provides optional assistance with Nora's core gameplay resources, including health, magic, stamina, shield strength, experience, gold, and combat effectiveness. It is designed to make exploration, platforming, weapon upgrades, and boss encounters more accessible while leaving the original game progression available.

**Target Audience:** Single-player users who want to reduce grinding, experiment with different weapon types and accessories, explore hidden areas more safely, or make platforming and boss encounters less demanding. Dungeons And Cat is intended for adults and contains sexual content, nudity, and non-consensual fantasy scenarios.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

- **No Attack Interruption** — Reduces the impact of enemy hits that would otherwise interrupt Nora's actions.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Use the game's normal movement, jumping, crouching, crawling, and dash controls. Unlimited stamina is useful for repeated dashes, while unlimited health helps during hazardous platforming sections and traversal through enemy-filled rooms.

### Tools & Inventory

The inventory contains weapons, consumables, accessories, and important items. Nora can use light weapons, heavy weapons, throwing weapons, and shields. Equip the desired loadout before activating combat options, and use the gold and upgrade features when visiting merchants or the blacksmith.

### World & Environment

Guild missions unlock access to dungeon areas. Explore side paths and hidden rooms to find rare items, health improvements, magic improvements, and optional encounters. The trainer can reduce the risk of losing progress while searching for secrets, but quest objectives and area progression should still be completed normally.

### Combat & Weapons

Combat centers on basic attacks, strong attacks, thrown weapons, shields, active skills, dodging, and stamina management. Use enhanced damage for faster enemy clears, unlimited stamina for mobility, and defensive options when learning boss patterns. Disable offensive features when you want to preserve the game's intended balance.
